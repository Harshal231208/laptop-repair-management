About RepairBench

A ticket-first approach to laptop repair — built so you never have to call in just to ask "is it done yet?"

Why we built it this way

Most repair shops run on sticky notes and phone calls. We wanted something a customer could actually check themselves.

Transparent by default

Every ticket has a visible status, timestamped history, and an honest estimate — nothing happens behind the counter that you can't see.

Technician accountability

Each job is assigned to a named technician with logged hours, so there's always a clear owner for your repair.

Built for any device

From a cracked hinge to a dead motherboard, every repair — regardless of brand or issue — moves through the same clear pipeline.

How a ticket moves

The same four stages, every time.

01

Received

Your ticket is logged the moment you submit it — device, issue, and priority all on record.

02

Diagnosing / Repairing

A technician is assigned and works the ticket. Status updates are logged as the repair progresses.

03

Ready for pickup

Once the work is done, your ticket flips to "Ready for Pickup" — check anytime, no phone call needed.
